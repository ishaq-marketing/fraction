The full workflow

From operational complexity to one connected journey

Scroll through a single visit exactly as it moves through eMedEMR. The same registration‑to‑payment lifecycle behind OpenEMR, now running as one managed pipeline instead of six disconnected systems.

Step 1 of 6

Registration

Name, date of birth, address, phone, email, emergency contact and insurance captured in one guided form. No re‑entry later in the process.

Step 2 of 6

Scheduling & check‑in

The visit is booked to the provider's calendar, identity is verified on arrival, and insurance eligibility is confirmed before the patient sits down.

Step 3 of 6

Clinical encounter

Chief complaint, history of present illness, examination findings, assessment, plan, medications and follow‑up. Documented in one structured note.

Step 4 of 6

ICD/CPT coding

Diagnosis and procedure codes are assigned and validated against the encounter before anything is sent to a payer.

Step 5 of 6

Claim submission

An electronic claim is generated, validated and transmitted through a clearinghouse we configured and maintain for you.

Step 6 of 6

Payment & reporting

Payments are matched and posted, patient statements go out for any balance, and every step becomes a report your team can act on.

Common questions

Before you schedule a demo

Is eMedEMR a fork of OpenEMR, or the real thing?
eMedEMR runs on the genuine open‑source OpenEMR engine. We add managed hosting, clearinghouse integration and support on top. Your clinical workflow doesn't change underneath you.
How long does migration from self‑hosted OpenEMR take?
Timelines depend on practice size and data volume. Secure PHI import and clearinghouse setup are handled by our team as part of onboarding.
Do you support multi‑location practices?
Yes. The Professional and Enterprise plans support multi‑site and role‑based access configuration across locations.
